A Weekend Checklist Before You Cancel Cable

Cancelling cable feels like a big step, but it goes smoothly when you spend one weekend checking a few basics first. Here is the short list we recommend.

One: test your internet during the evening, when the neighborhood is online. If speeds drop sharply after 7 pm, talk to your provider or move the TV to a wired connection before relying on streaming.

Two: check every TV in the house. Older smart TVs may not get current apps, and a streaming stick is often cheaper than fighting an outdated system.

Three: list the local channels and sports you cannot miss, and confirm how you will watch each one. This single step avoids most regrets after the switch.

Four: think about the whole household. A simple remote and a clear channel guide matter as much as picture quality when several people share the TV.

Five: keep the cable box for a week or two while you try the new setup side by side.
