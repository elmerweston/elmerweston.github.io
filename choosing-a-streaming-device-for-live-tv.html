Choosing a Streaming Device for Live TV

Most streaming devices look alike on paper, yet they behave very differently once you watch live channels for a few hours. Live TV keeps a constant stream running, so the weak spots of a cheap device show up quickly.

Memory is the first thing to check. Devices with 1 GB of RAM tend to reload apps and stutter when switching channels; 2 GB or more keeps the guide and the player responsive.

Wi-Fi support matters next. A device with Wi-Fi 6 or at least a good 5 GHz radio holds a steadier connection, and a model with an Ethernet port, or a cheap USB adapter, removes the most common cause of buffering entirely.

App support is the last filter. Make sure the player you plan to use is available and still updated on that platform; an outdated app on an older box is often the real reason channels freeze.
